Privacy Policy

This page explains how Keyan Health App handles information when you connect supported health services, including WHOOP.

Privacy information Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Keyan Health App is a personal health-tracking application intended to help its owner view and understand health and fitness information in one place. This policy describes the information the application may access and how that information is intended to be used.

2. Information accessed

If you authorize a supported integration such as WHOOP, the application may access information permitted by the scopes you approve. Depending on those permissions and the service's API, this may include:

  • Recovery information, including recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate.
  • Sleep information, such as sleep duration and sleep performance.
  • Daily activity-cycle information, including strain and heart-rate summaries.
  • Workout and activity information.
  • Basic profile and body-measurement information, where authorized.

3. How information is used

Information accessed through an authorized integration is used to support the application's personal health-tracking features, such as:

  • Displaying health and fitness metrics.
  • Reviewing historical trends.
  • Producing personal summaries and visualizations.

The application does not use health information for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Information may be stored in the application's database to support historical views and analytics. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be applied to protect stored information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-party services

The application may connect to third-party services such as WHOOP. When you authorize an integration, the relevant service processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Access by this application is limited by the permissions you grant and the capabilities of the service's API.

For WHOOP's privacy information, visit WHOOP's Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing of information

The application is intended for personal use. Personal health information is not sold. It is not shared with third parties for their advertising purposes. Any third-party processing necessary to operate the application should be disclosed here before the application is made available to others.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information may be retained for as long as it is needed for the application's tracking and historical-analysis features. To request deletion of information or ask a privacy question, use the contact details below. You can also revoke the application's access through the connected service, where that option is available. Revoking access may stop future data retrieval; it may not automatically delete information already stored by the application.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application's features, integrations, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
