# Privacy Policy – Keyan Health App **Last updated:** November 13, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Keyan Health App is a personal health tracking application designed to help users monitor and understand their health and fitness information. ## 2. Information We Collect The application may access the following information through authorized integrations such as WHOOP: - Recovery metrics, including HRV and resting heart rate. - Sleep duration and sleep performance. - Daily strain and heart rate information. - Workout and activity information. - Basic profile and body measurement information. ## 3. How We Use Information The collected information is used exclusively to: - Display health and fitness metrics. - Analyze historical health trends. - Generate personal health insights. - Provide visualizations and reports. ## 4. Data Storage and Security Health information is stored in the application's database for personal tracking and analytics. Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information. ## 5. Third-Party Services The application integrates with WHOOP APIs. Data access is subject to the permissions granted by the user and WHOOP's applicable policies. ## 6. Data Sharing We do not sell personal health information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes. ## 7. Data Deletion Users can request deletion of their stored information by contacting the application owner. ## 8. Contact For privacy-related questions, contact: keyan.health@gmail.com
